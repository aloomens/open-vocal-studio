Algorithms (published research, no code copied)
  Pitch detection: A. de Cheveigne and H. Kawahara, "YIN, a fundamental frequency
    estimator for speech and music", JASA 111(4), 2002.
  Pitch shifting: E. Moulines and F. Charpentier, "Pitch-synchronous waveform processing
    techniques for text-to-speech synthesis using diphones", Speech Communication 9, 1990
    (TD-PSOLA).
  Key detection: C. Krumhansl and E. Kessler, "Tracing the dynamic changes in perceived
    tonal organization in a spatial representation of musical keys", Psychological
    Review 89, 1982 (key profiles).
  Noise estimate: R. Martin, "Noise power spectral density estimation based on optimal
    smoothing and minimum statistics", IEEE Trans. Speech and Audio Processing 9, 2001.
  Noise reduction gain: Y. Ephraim and D. Malah, "Speech enhancement using a minimum
    mean-square error short-time spectral amplitude estimator", IEEE Trans. ASSP 32, 1984
    (decision-directed a-priori SNR).
  De-room: K. Lebart, J.-M. Boucher and P. N. Denbigh, "A new method based on spectral
    subtraction for speech dereverberation", Acta Acustica 87, 2001.
  Reverb: feedback delay network with frequency-dependent absorption per line after
    J.-M. Jot and A. Chaigne, "Digital Delay Networks for Designing Artificial
    Reverberators", AES 90th Convention, 1991.
