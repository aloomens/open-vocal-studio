Generated by `VocalTest.exe --lab` (Tests/VocalTest.cpp), in the spirit of THE_LAB: latency reported vs measured, nulls, true peak, clicks, and what each processor actually does to test signals.

Nulls are measured from 200 Hz to 12 kHz against the input sent through the same 20 Hz rumble filter. In Mix mode at 44.1 and 96 kHz the neural path runs at 48 kHz, so the audio is resampled (passband to 20 kHz, about -100 dB stopband); that, not the processing, sets those two nulls.
