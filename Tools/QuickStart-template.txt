OPEN VOCAL STUDIO {VERSION} - Quick Start (Windows 64-bit)
==========================================================

What's in this folder
---------------------
  Standalone\Open Vocal Studio.exe   The standalone app (sing through your ASIO sound card)
  VST3\Open Vocal Studio.vst3        The plugin for Cubase or another DAW (a folder - keep it whole)
  Install VST3.bat                   Copies the plugin into C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
  README.md                          Full manual: signal chain, every control, tests
  MEASUREMENTS.md                    Measured latency, nulls, true peak, what each part does
  LICENSE.txt                        GNU Affero General Public License version 3
  THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt            Licenses for included components (and the files named there)

Runs on any 64-bit Windows 10/11 PC. There's nothing else to install.

New in 1.3.0
------------
* Vocal Studio is now called Open Vocal Studio. It's the same plugin (same ID): your projects
  and presets load as before. Install VST3.bat removes the old "Vocal Studio.vst3"; if you
  copy the plugin by hand, delete the old one, or your DAW sees two copies.
* The standalone app asks for its audio device setup once (its settings use the new name).
* A look shared with Clean Tone Studio and Open Mastering Studio: OPEN VOCAL in white and
  STUDIO in blue, underlined section titles, knob values with units ("60 %", "+1.5 dB"),
  and buttons that do something once in normal case (Use Detected, Flat, Open...,
  Save Processed...).
* The sound is unchanged.

New in 1.2.6
------------
* The standalone app can play a recording through Open Vocal Studio and save it processed: use
  the FILE strip at the bottom (OPEN..., PLAY, LOOP, SAVE PROCESSED). WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3
  and Ogg files; the saved WAV lines up with the original. LIVE still sings through the
  sound card as before.
* The A/B button is now a PROCESSED | ORIGINAL switch: the lit one is what you hear.
* USE DETECTED confirms the key it set, and is greyed out until a key has been detected.
* The Doubler goes further: turning it up raises the copies' level, timing drift and slight
  detuning together. Low settings sound much as before.
* Your projects and presets load as before.

Measuring with THE_LAB: set Denoise AND Doubler to 0 - see README.md.

New in 1.2.5
------------
* New Consonant knob (TONE section, K meter under DYNAMICS): softens the click of hard
  consonants (K, T, hard C). It starts at 0 %, so your projects sound as before; try 30-50 %
  on a clicky take.
* Reverb has a lit OFF / PLATE / ROOM switch, and the controls of a section that's off are
  dimmed (reverb, delay, pitch correction).
* The version is shown under the VOCAL STUDIO title and in the standalone app's title bar.
* The first phrase after a stretch of room tone is no longer squashed by the compressor.
* Pitch correction leaves whistles and tones above the singing range alone.
* Your DAW now shows the preset you picked in the plugin window.
* Your projects and presets load as before.

Measuring with THE_LAB: set Denoise to 0. The denoiser is built to remove noise, steady
tones and sweeps, which are THE_LAB's test signals - see README.md.

New in 1.2.1
------------
* Track mode no longer fades long held notes: its denoiser now protects a sung note the way
  Mix mode does.
* Pitch correction stays steady on noisy recordings (it could wander by up to 20 cents).
* The rider and compressor follow the voice when it gets quieter for good (a quieter take, or
  the Input turned down); before, the compressor could stop compressing until the voice got
  loud again.
* Your projects and presets load as before.

New in 1.2.0
------------
* Stopping and starting your DAW no longer changes the vocal's level for the next few seconds.
* The rider no longer turns up fading phrase ends and breaths.
* A constant buzz or hum is removed again (1.1.0 could protect it as if it were a voice);
  long held notes keep more of their level.
* Your projects and presets load as before.

New in 1.1.0
------------
* The neural denoiser now runs inside the plugin itself (no ONNX Runtime DLL), frame by frame:
  Mix-mode latency drops from about 0.64 s to about 170 ms, and the plugin never allocates
  memory while playing and gives exactly the same result on every run and buffer size.
* Long, steady notes and tones are no longer thinned by the denoiser or the resonance control.
* Pitch correction leaves the first 60 ms of each note alone and drifts back into exact
  alignment with the input after a correction (fixes an inverted-looking sweep in THE_LAB).
* No DC offset from pitch correction (the rumble filter now comes after it).
* Your projects and presets load as before.

First steps
-----------
1. Insert Open Vocal Studio on your lead vocal track.
2. Choose a preset (Natural Polish is the default).
3. Click ANALYZE, play 10-30 seconds of the vocal, click ANALYZE again: it sets the tone EQ
   and the key for pitch correction.
4. Use PROCESSED | ORIGINAL to hear the untouched input at the same loudness.

Mix mode vs Track mode
----------------------
Mix (best quality) uses the neural denoiser and has about 170 ms of latency, which your DAW
compensates automatically - use it for mixing. While RECORDING through the plugin, switch to
Track (low latency, 6 ms; 55 ms with pitch correction on) or use the "Tracking" preset.
